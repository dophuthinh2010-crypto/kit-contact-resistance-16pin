Model assumptions

Start resistance 2.2–3.5 mΩ (datasheet: 5 mΩ max new). Wear R = R₀ + A·(N/500)^1.6 with A ≈ 3 mΩ per pin (one weaker pin), plus random fretting spikes that grow more likely with cycles. Noise: ACS712 ≈ 4 mV rms (averaged), INA333 offset and noise after calibration, ADS1115 quantisation. Replace the wear model with your logged data once the kit runs.
